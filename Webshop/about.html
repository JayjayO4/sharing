Who are we?

Located in the bustling Wan Chai district, the Wan Chai Computer Centre is a go-to destination for tech enthusiasts, gamers, and professionals in Hong Kong. This multi-floor electronics mall is packed with shops offering a wide range of gadgets, from the latest laptops, PCs, and smartphones to gaming gear, accessories, and components for DIY builders. The center is known for its competitive prices, with vendors selling everything from brand-name electronics to niche tech products. Whether you're looking for high-performance gaming rigs, rare hardware parts, or just browsing for deals, Wan Chai Computer Centre has something for everyone. Beyond hardware, the mall also features repair services, software shops, and stalls selling peripherals like keyboards, mice, and VR headsets.
